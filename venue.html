Things to do

Leavenworth

You're welcome to come earlier or stay longer and enjoy the perks of Leavenworth. Fun fact: Leavenworth is derived from the German "lebenswert", which means livable or worth living.

  • Leavenworth Adventure Park — their rollercoaster is fun and you get to control the speed. You must reserve your time in advance — ride slots fill up 2–3 weeks ahead.
  • Tube Leavenworth — relaxing river float
  • Rocky Pond Estate Winery — wine tasting
  • Leavenworth Golf Course
  • Miniature Golf at Icicle Junction
  • Enzian Falls Putting Course — not the same as mini golf; more of a place to practice putting
  • Explore Leavenworth Riverside Park
  • Leavenworth Reindeer Farm
  • The Springs Spa
